### P7.1 Turn every incident into a rule, a template change or a recorded "won't fix"
- **Rule:** At the end of the project, take each "Where the AI was wrong" entry from `AI-Agents.md` and turn it into a rule, a check, a template change or a decision not to fix it, with the reason.
- **Why (incident):** In this repository's own log, the first version of the decision-log rule did not see a heading that grouped three decisions and let all three escape every check; that miss became the grouped-heading check in `checks/check_docs.py`.
- **How to check:** For each "Where the AI was wrong" entry in `AI-Agents.md`, ask: is there a matching rule, check, template change or "won't fix" decision?
- **Enforced by:** human gate
